After deploying your Hydrogen storefront to Oxygen, you'll need to connect a custom domain so customers can access your store at your own URL (e.g., `yourdomain.com`). This guide walks through adding the domain in Shopify, pointing it to your Hydrogen storefront, configuring DNS records, and giving checkout its own subdomain.

<Note>
  This guide uses **Cloudflare** as the DNS provider for examples, but the same DNS records apply to any provider (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Route 53, etc.). The key difference is where you create the records and how you disable proxying.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- A Hydrogen storefront deployed to [Oxygen](/guides/deployment/oxygen), with a **public** production environment. Development stores and trial plans can't make an environment public.
- A registered domain name
- Access to your domain's DNS management panel

<Note>
  Hosting somewhere else ([Docker](/guides/deployment/docker), [Cloudflare Workers](/guides/deployment/workers))? Point your primary domain at that host following its documentation. Only the checkout subdomain (Step 4 below) points to Shopify.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Add Domain in Shopify Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open domain settings">
    Go to **Shopify Admin** → **Settings** → **Domains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your domain">
    Click **Connect existing domain** and enter your domain (e.g., `yourdomain.com`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Point Domain to Hydrogen

Once the domain appears in your domain list:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your domain">
    Click on your domain name in the domains list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the target">
    Change the target from **Online Store** to your production **Hydrogen** storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it primary">
    Set the domain type to **Primary**. Do the same for any other domain that should serve the storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  This step is critical. If the domain still points to the Online Store, visitors will see the Liquid theme instead of your Hydrogen app.
</Warning>

## Step 3 — Configure DNS Records

Log in to your DNS provider's dashboard and create the following records. Delete any other `A` and `AAAA` records on the root domain: Shopify allows only one of each.

### Root Domain

| Type   | Name | Value                  | Proxy (Cloudflare) |
| ------ | ---- | ---------------------- | ------------------ |
| `A`    | `@`  | `23.227.38.65`         | **OFF** (DNS only) |
| `AAAA` | `@`  | `2620:0127:f00f:5::`   | **OFF** (DNS only) |

If your provider rejects the shortened IPv6 address, enter it in full: `2620:0127:f00f:0005:0000:0000:0000:0000`.

### WWW Subdomain

| Type    | Name  | Value                 | Proxy (Cloudflare) |
| ------- | ----- | --------------------- | ------------------ |
| `CNAME` | `www` | `shops.myshopify.com` | **OFF** (DNS only) |

Keep the default TTL.

<Warning>
  **Cloudflare users**: set **every** record that points to Shopify to **DNS only** (grey cloud). Shopify doesn't support Cloudflare's proxy, so a proxied record blocks the connection and SSL provisioning. Also delete any wildcard record that points to Shopify, and make sure your `CAA` records (if any) allow Let's Encrypt, Google and SSL.com.
</Warning>

## Step 4 — Add a Checkout Subdomain

Checkout stays on Shopify, so it needs its own subdomain, such as `checkout.yourdomain.com`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the subdomain">
    In **Settings** → **Domains**, click **Connect existing domain** and enter the subdomain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add its DNS record">
    Create a `CNAME` record for the subdomain (for example `checkout`) pointing to `shops.myshopify.com`, with the proxy **OFF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its target">
    Set the subdomain's target to **Online Store** and its type to **Primary**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 5 — Verify the Connection

After updating your DNS records:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for propagation">
    DNS changes typically take **5–30 minutes** to propagate, though in some cases it can take up to 48 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify in Shopify">
    Go back to **Shopify Admin** → **Settings** → **Domains** and check that your domain shows as **Connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SSL certificate">
    Shopify will **automatically provision** an SSL certificate for your domain. This may take a few additional minutes after DNS verification completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Domain shows as 'Not connected' in Shopify">
    - Double-check the `A` (`23.227.38.65`) and `AAAA` (`2620:0127:f00f:5::`) records on `@`, and the `www` `CNAME` to `shops.myshopify.com`
    - Ensure DNS proxy is disabled (Cloudflare users: grey cloud, not orange)
    - Wait up to 48 hours for full DNS propagation
    - Try removing and re-adding the domain in Shopify
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SSL certificate not provisioning">
    - Verify that Cloudflare proxy is **OFF** (DNS only / grey cloud). This is the most common cause
    - Ensure no other `A` or `AAAA` records exist on the root domain, and no wildcard record points to Shopify
    - If the domain has `CAA` records, make sure they allow Let's Encrypt, Google and SSL.com
    - Check that your domain registrar hasn't locked DNS changes
    - Wait up to 24 hours — Shopify retries SSL provisioning periodically
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Site shows Liquid theme instead of Hydrogen">
    - Go to **Shopify Admin** → **Settings** → **Domains** → click your domain
    - Confirm the target is set to your **Hydrogen** storefront, not **Online Store**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ERR_TOO_MANY_REDIRECTS error">
    - This usually means a Shopify record is still proxied through Cloudflare (orange cloud)
    - Set every record that points to Shopify to **DNS only**. Shopify doesn't support Cloudflare's proxy, so changing the SSL/TLS mode is not a fix
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
